# Alice Harvest Startup Gate

Alice operates as the command-oriented run gatekeeper.

1. Verify operator, facility, station, and writable local data root.
2. Verify device identity, firmware identity, calibration status, and evidence truth class.
3. Select New, Continue, or Recovery.
4. Require harvest-run ID and preserve external tracking identifiers when supplied.
5. Capture room/zone/rack/source context, measurement stage, and weight purpose.
6. Register the cultivar roster with raw strain names and separately normalized cultivar names.
7. Confirm container/tare policy, automatic or manual capture, stability profile, and expected maximum capacity.
8. Present a start-manifest review and block unresolved required fields.

Alice may block failed application gates. Alice may not invent identifiers, bypass failed persistence, rewrite immutable events, or declare legal/regulatory compliance.
