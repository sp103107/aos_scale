# Operator Runbook

1. Complete Alice startup and local write preflight. 2. Connect and identify the device. 3. Confirm calibration and tare/container policy. 4. Start or recover the run. 5. Scan barcode. 6. Wait for stable weight. 7. Automatic commit or Confirm & Continue. 8. Verify success feedback and next-barcode state. Errors mean not saved.
